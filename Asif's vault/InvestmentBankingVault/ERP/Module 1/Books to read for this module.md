# 📚 Module 1 — Markets & Fundamentals

### **ONE clear, structured study order**

---

## 🔹 STEP 1 — Big-Picture Energy System (WHY markets exist)

### 📘 **The Quest – Daniel Yergin**

**Read first. Always.**

**Why this first**

- Gives you **energy as a system**, not as trades
    
- Explains:
    
    - Demand drivers
        
    - Energy security
        
    - Geopolitics
        
    - Transition dynamics
        
- Prevents you from becoming a “screen-level Endur dev”
    

**How to read**

- Not technical
    
- 30–40 pages/day
    
- Goal: _mental model_, not memorization
    

✅ Outcome:  
You understand **why oil, gas, power markets even exist** and why volatility is structural.

---

## 🔹 STEP 2 — Energy Market Structure (WHAT markets look like)

### 📕 **Energy Markets – Vincent Kaminski**

**This is your core Module 1 textbook.**

**Why**

- Written by a practitioner
    
- Covers:
    
    - Oil, gas, power, emissions
        
    - Physical vs financial
        
    - Transport, storage, constraints
        
- Directly aligns with ERP syllabus language
    

**What to read (Module 1 only)**

- Market participants
    
- Physical flows
    
- Benchmarks & hubs
    
- Spot vs forward markets
    
- Role of exchanges
    

📌 Ignore heavy quant sections for now.

✅ Outcome:  
You can explain **how energy actually moves** and **how prices are formed**.

---

## 🔹 STEP 3 — Trading & Risk Framing (HOW desks think)

### 📗 **Energy Trading & Risk Management – Iris Mack**

**Read after Kaminski, not before.**

**Why**

- Connects markets → instruments → risk
    
- Introduces:
    
    - Hedging logic
        
    - Portfolio thinking
        
    - Risk motivations
        

**What to focus on**

- Physical vs financial trades
    
- Hedging motivations
    
- Risk types (market, credit, operational)
    
- Trade lifecycle intuition
    

📌 This book bridges **business thinking → ETRM systems**.

✅ Outcome:  
You now think like **a junior trader / risk analyst**, not just an engineer.

---

## 🔹 STEP 4 — ERP Framing (WHY ERP syllabus exists)

### 📙 **ERP Exam Handbook / GARP ERP Study Notes**

_(Official ERP material — mandatory)_

**Why now**

- Now the ERP syllabus will **make sense**
    
- You won’t be memorizing abstract definitions
    

**Focus only on Module 1 topics**

- Market participants
    
- Physical vs financial markets
    
- Commodity characteristics
    
- Energy-specific features (storability, transport, seasonality)
    

📌 Do NOT try to “finish ERP” here.  
We are **aligning mental models**, not chasing certification.

✅ Outcome:  
You understand **what ERP is testing and why**.
