# 📌 1. Trader Cheat Sheet: Weather → Energy Prices

## 🎯 Purpose

A **fast mental reference** for traders, analysts, and ETRM professionals to translate weather information into **price intuition**.

---

## 🌡️ Temperature → Demand Mapping

### Electricity

- **Hotter-than-normal summer**
    
    - ↑ Air conditioning load
        
    - ↑ Gas-fired peakers
        
    - ↑ Power prices (esp. on-peak)
        
- **Milder summer**
    
    - ↓ Load
        
    - ↓ Gas burn
        
    - ↓ Power prices
        

### Natural Gas

- **Cold winter**
    
    - ↑ Residential & commercial heating
        
    - ↑ Gas withdrawals
        
    - ↑ Basis volatility
        
- **Hot summer (US)**
    
    - ↑ Power sector gas burn
        
    - ↑ Storage draw risk
        

### Heating Oil

- **Cold winter**
    
    - ↑ Demand in US Northeast, Europe, Asia
        
    - Strong regional price sensitivity

## 🌪️ Event-Based Supply Shocks

|Event|Immediate Impact|Market Effect|
|---|---|---|
|Hurricane|Offshore shutdowns|↑ Gas & crude|
|Flooding|Barge disruption|↑ Coal / refined products|
|Drought|Low hydro output|↑ Power prices|
|Fog (ports)|Shipping delays|Local price spikes|

---

## 🧠 Model Bias Awareness (Very Important)

- **GFS flips frequently** → knee-jerk market reactions
    
- **ECMWF stability** → slower but stronger conviction
    
- Markets often trade **model dominance**, not truth
    

> Sometimes you trade _what traders believe_, not what will happen.

---

## ⚠️ Common Trading Mistakes

- Overreacting to **long-range (>7 day)** forecasts
    
- Ignoring infrastructure constraints
    
- Treating seasonal outlooks as certainties
    
- Forgetting **demand destruction** after hurricanes
