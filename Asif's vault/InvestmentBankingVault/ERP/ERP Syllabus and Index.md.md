## Module 1 — Markets & Fundamentals

**Enterprise / System Concepts:**

- ERP fundamentals: operational vs financial reality, event-driven vs state-driven systems
    
- Master data vs transactional data, reference vs configuration data
    
- Enterprise process thinking: FO → MO → BO workflows
    
- Single source of truth, audit trails, versioning
    

**Trading / Business Concepts:**

- Physical vs financial markets
    
- Market structure: power, gas, oil, coal, emissions
    
- Supply-demand dynamics, seasonal patterns
    
- Key pricing benchmarks and indices (e.g., ICE, NYMEX, Platts)
    
- Role of market participants and interconnections
    

**Outcome:** Understand both **how the system models market reality** and **why trades exist in the market**.
