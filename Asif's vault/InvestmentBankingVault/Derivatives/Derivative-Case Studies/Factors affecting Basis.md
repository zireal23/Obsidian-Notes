
## Factors That Affect Basis Differentials 🌾📈

### What is Basis?

- In **natural gas**, _basis_ = price difference between two delivery points.
    
- In **basis swaps**, you trade this difference.
    
- The **actual basis differential** changes if:
    
    1. Value of gas at Location A changes relative to Location B (futures delivery point).
        
    2. Futures price changes while physical prices remain the same.
        
    3. Fixed physical price changes while futures remain the same.
        
    4. A combination of changes in futures and physical prices that do not correlate.
        

---

### Key Drivers of Basis Differentials

✅ **Supply and demand fundamentals** at:

- The _local physical market_ (basis location).
    
- The _futures contract delivery point_.
    

✅ Understanding **futures price drivers**:

- Technical market moves.
    
- Local demand-supply driven moves.
    

✅ **Weather**:

- Current and anticipated weather patterns at _each location_ are the **most important factors**.
    
- Different weather patterns → different impacts on local prices.
    

---

### Example: Permian Basis Swap

**Scenario**:

- Trader sells _Permian basis swap_ at **L3D - $0.30**.
    
- Expects **colder weather** in the Northeast → **higher demand** → **futures price at Henry Hub rises by $0.10**.
    

**Question**: What happens to Permian basis differential?

---

#### Forecast Table:

|Permian Price Change|Likelihood|
|---|---|
|Fall > $0.10|5%|
|Fall $0.01–$0.10|10%|
|Unchanged|60%|
|Rise $0.01–$0.10|20%|
|Rise > $0.10|5%|

---

### Interpretation:

- Only **5% chance** Permian prices rise faster than Henry Hub → **tightening basis**.
    
- **60% chance** Permian prices remain unchanged while Henry Hub rises → **basis widens** to **L3D - $0.40**.
    
- Trader expects to **buy back at L3D - $0.40** after selling at **L3D - $0.30** → **$0.10 profit**.
    

---

### Key Takeaways for Basis Swap Traders:

✅ Track **local fundamentals** + **futures fundamentals**.  
✅ Watch **weather patterns** at _both locations_.  
✅ Realize you are _trading two markets simultaneously_.  
✅ Anticipate **basis widening or tightening** based on relative price moves.  
✅ Use **probabilistic thinking** to assess trade profit potential.
