## 1. Why Financial Institutions Participate in Commodities Trading

### Key Question

Why do banks and financial institutions participate in a business rooted in physical goods?

### Core Themes

- Commodities are **real-economy risk**, not abstract instruments
    
- Financial institutions intermediate:
    
    - Price risk
        
    - Timing risk
        
    - Liquidity risk
        
- Commodities desks monetize:
    
    - Information asymmetry
        
    - Balance sheet strength
        
    - Risk transformation
        

### Important Contrast

- Difference between:
    
    - Speculation
        
    - Market-making
        
    - Client facilitation
        
    - Structured risk transfer
