# **Level 3 – Organizational Reality & Operations**

### _How trading outputs become enterprise responsibility and physical execution_

## **Level Objective**

Understand how data produced by trading, pricing, risk, and PnL systems is **consumed, validated, challenged, and acted upon** across the organization, and how **operational execution ultimately determines realized outcomes**.

This level deliberately broadens the lens beyond core trading systems, focusing on **people, processes, controls, and physical reality**.

---

## **Module 7 – Organizational Stakeholders, Controls & Data Consumers**

### _Who uses trading data and why_

**Objective**  
Explain how trading outputs flow into control, finance, legal, risk, and back-office functions, and why these teams care deeply about accuracy, timing, and explainability.

**Scope**

- Independent Price Verification (IPV) and valuation control
    
- Accounting and finance (front-office vs accounting PnL, close processes)
    
- XVA functions (CVA, DVA, FVA – conceptual)
    
- Market risk and credit risk teams
    
- Legal, compliance, and contract management
    
- Reference data and counterparty maintenance
    
- Settlements, confirmations, and payments
    

**Key Insight**  
Trading numbers are not “final” until independent teams accept them.

---

## **Module 8 – Operations, Logistics & Physical Execution**

### _Where commercial intent meets real-world constraints_

**Objective**  
Explain how physical execution, logistics, and operational decisions determine whether commercial value is realized.

**Scope**

- Scheduling and nominations
    
- Logistics and infrastructure constraints
    
- Inventory management and reconciliation
    
- Settlements, invoicing, and cash flow timing
    
- Operational risk and exception management
    
- Optimization vs feasibility trade-offs
    

**Key Insight**  
PnL is theoretical until operations and cash flow succeed.


---

## **End State of Level 3**

By the end of this level, participants can:

- Identify who consumes trading, risk, and PnL data across the enterprise
    
- Understand why controls, validation, and disputes exist
    
- Appreciate operational complexity behind “simple” trades
    
- See how organizational structure and physical execution impact financial results
